# KnightLedger verification — October 26, 2026

## Current release

No-card assisted operation uses a private, nonexpiring Vercel Blob ledger, conditional writes, and the existing LEGION chat. Model API calls are disabled. The owner transfers prepared work packs and approves quests, evidence decisions and grounded proclamations. This is an explicit operating workflow, not a mock AI backend. The funded automatic upgrade is documented in docs/ENABLE_FULL_AI.md.

Public wording and navigation were rebuilt around quests, local requests, proof and recognition. Local requests require no passport. The first-proof flow creates a Squire passport; ordinary browsing and completing a quest require no registration. The science news section, special science filter, decorative taglines and standalone enrollment panel were removed. Six astronomy quests use the same format as the eight other quests.

## Limits

The whole-ledger pilot adapter is bounded to 2 MB and the account’s included hosting/storage/operations allowance. Hobby’s included Blob limits are 1 GB storage, 10,000 simple operations, 2,000 advanced operations and 10 GB transfer monthly; exhaustion can pause service. The normalized connected-account tools do not expose the actual subscription plan. No unlimited free service is promised.

Required live variable names: BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN, SESSION_SECRET, OPERATOR_SECRET, CRON_SECRET, KNIGHTLEDGER_STORAGE, KNIGHTLEDGER_AI_MODE, KL_NAMESPACE, KNIGHTLEDGER_PRIVATE_PREVIEW, KNIGHTLEDGER_PUBLIC_SITE, AI_PAUSED. No paid AI credential or database claim is required by assisted Blob operation.
