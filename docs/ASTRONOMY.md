The batch sizes and effort estimates are KnightLedger's editorial completion standards, not requirements or promises made by the receiving organizations. Negative/uncertain classifications and project-provided calibration subjects can be useful. Tutorial replay, data requests, unsubmitted plots and a claimed discovery alone do not complete a quest. Every new useful batch or observation may count again; the same submission cannot earn both archival-analysis and telescope-observing credit.

## What is actually available

Galaxy Zoo's current classifier advertises Rubin work. Do not describe its current subjects as Euclid or Webb images without verifying the selected workflow. Planet Hunters TESS exposes its live transit workflow. Dark Energy Explorers exposes its current detection/noise workflow. These observations verify the advertised participation paths, not a guarantee that a particular subject queue will remain available. No classifications or scientific observations were submitted during this research.

Exoplanet Watch documents both archive-data and telescope routes. The archive service requests an email and limits requests to once per 48 hours. Its browser EXOTIC route uses Google Colab, and AAVSO submission needs a separate free account/observer code. The observing route requires suitable tracking/imaging equipment and several hours of data; a casual telescope photo is insufficient. Both routes must produce a valid submitted light curve, not merely a practice plot.

AAVSO's current getting-started guide supports visual observations, including appropriate naked-eye targets, with its charts, accurate times and comparison data. Site/equipment metadata requested by a receiving research project stays there; KnightLedger evidence should redact exact observing-site coordinates and personal account details.

## Preview and promotion

`KNIGHTLEDGER_PRIVATE_PREVIEW=true` is configured only on the isolated staging project. It shows only explicitly marked editorial astronomy drafts, never arbitrary local research drafts or private petitions. Turn it off before any approved public production release. Draft details have no undertaking/proof action, and server mutations reject attempts to bypass that gate.

`scripts/prepare-astronomy.ts` is idempotent and adds drafts only. It does not approve, publish, mutate accepted deeds or alter ranks. The owner can review the complete source-backed cards in `/steward`; substantive edits invalidate approval. Sources are due for recheck November 1, 2026.

Selection prioritizes useful volunteer contributions and repeat participation. Research organizations are identified as independent projects; no charitable registration, partnership, endorsement or impact is invented.
